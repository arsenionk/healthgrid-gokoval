# HealthGrid – GoKoval Solar Energy Platform

HealthGrid is a data-driven solar energy and monitoring solution designed to provide reliable electricity for healthcare, education, and community facilities in low-resource settings such as Timor-Leste.

## Overview
HealthGrid integrates solar PV systems, battery storage, and digital monitoring tools to ensure continuous operation of critical services, including vaccine refrigeration, clinics, and schools.

## Key Features
- Solar PV + battery backup for reliable power
- Real-time monitoring of energy generation and system performance
- Fault detection and maintenance support
- Energy usage tracking (GoKoval Meter prototype)
- Designed for low-connectivity environments (offline capability)

## Problem
Many energy systems fail due to lack of monitoring, maintenance, and accountability, leading to repeated infrastructure investments without long-term impact.

## Solution
HealthGrid combines energy infrastructure with digital monitoring and local capacity building to ensure long-term reliability and sustainability.

## Status
Prototype / early-stage implementation with real-world deployments in Timor-Leste.
